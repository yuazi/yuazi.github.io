I shoot 35mm film as a hobby. A lab develops and scans each roll, and some of the frames end up on my [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/s.f_yuu/) as collages cut across several slides. The collages below are built the same way, from six rolls shot between December 2022 and December 2024, and grouped by the light they were taken in.

Each collage scrolls sideways. On a phone it moves one slide at a time, the way a carousel does on Instagram.
