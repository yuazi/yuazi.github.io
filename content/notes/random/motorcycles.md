Motorcycles just clicked for me. On a bike I can't think about anything except the road in front of me, and I don't get that kind of focus anywhere else.

## My bike

**Husqvarna Vitpilen 701.** I've had this one for a while now and still love it. The 692cc single-cylinder thumper has a character multi-cylinders don't: it's punchy and sounds angry at low RPMs, in a good way. The café racer silhouette turns heads too. It's not the most practical bike, but it's easily the most fun to ride every day. The perfect daily driver ^^

## Bikes I want in the future

- **Honda CBR 600RR.** The underseat exhaust is one of the best design decisions in motorcycle history: it keeps the tail clean and the sound is incredible.
- **Yamaha R6.** Probably the most rewarding bike to push hard on a track. The inline-four screams past 10k RPM and begs to be wrung out. It's not friendly for commuting, but it's a weapon on twisty roads.
- **BMW F800.** Understated and dead reliable, a bike you could ride across continents without thinking twice. I appreciate the engineering more the older I get.
