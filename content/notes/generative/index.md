The math behind the moving things on this site: dynamical systems, strange attractors and the noise, fractal and simulation techniques I use for visuals. The [[notes/random/lorenz-attractor|Lorenz attractor essay]] is where this started, and [[work/attractormediapipe|AttractorMediaPipe]] is where it ends up as a project.

---

## Chaos

- [[notes/generative/dynamical-systems|(y-) Dynamical Systems Basics]]: phase space, fixed points, stability and why chaos needs three dimensions.
- [[notes/generative/numerical-integration|(y-) Numerical Integration (Euler to RK4)]]: how simulations step forward in time and what the step size costs you.
- [[notes/generative/strange-attractors|(y-) Strange Attractors Beyond Lorenz]]: Rössler, Thomas, Aizawa, Halvorsen and the 2D Clifford and De Jong maps.
- [[notes/generative/logistic-map-and-bifurcations|(y-) The Logistic Map and Bifurcations]]: one line of code, period doubling and the Feigenbaum constant.
- [[notes/generative/lyapunov-exponents|(y-) Lyapunov Exponents]]: measuring how fast nearby trajectories separate, and how far ahead you can predict.
- [[notes/generative/fractals|(y-) Fractals and Fractal Dimension]]: Mandelbrot and Julia sets, escape-time coloring and box counting.

## Generative Techniques

- [[notes/generative/noise-functions|(y-) Noise Functions (Perlin, Simplex, fBm)]]: smooth randomness, octaves and domain warping.
- [[notes/generative/reaction-diffusion|(y-) Reaction-Diffusion (Gray-Scott)]]: two chemicals, a Laplacian and spots, stripes and coral.
- [[notes/generative/flow-fields-and-particles|(y-) Flow Fields and Particle Systems]]: curl noise, advection and trails.
